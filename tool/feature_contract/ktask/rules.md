Feature contract (this project, non-negotiable): the application's must-have features are listed in test/contract/must_have_features.json and proven by test/contract/must_have_features_test.dart against the real shipped widget tree; its user-facing controls are inventoried in test/contract/ui_surface/. No task removes, hides, disables, or makes harder to reach any registered feature or inventoried control unless its Goal explicitly names that feature or control and orders its removal. A redesign, rewrite, refactor, dependency or Flutter upgrade is never such an order. Never delete, skip, weaken or narrow a contract test, a registry entry or a platform target, and never edit an inventory by hand: regenerate it with `flutter test test/contract/ui_surface_test.dart --update-goldens`. A renamed or moved control is recorded in test/contract/approved_removals.json with "replaced_by"; an "owner_approval" entry may only quote the task Goal that ordered the removal. New user-facing capability gets a registry entry, a contract for every platform it ships on, and a regenerated inventory. Do not regenerate golden images to clear a failure without inspecting the difference; a golden outside tolerance is a real visual change that must be either intended by the Goal or fixed.
